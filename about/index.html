BIOGRAPHY

A little about me.

I am Ziyo Abdullazizov, an International Relations student at Ege University and a Türkiye Scholarships recipient, interested in diplomacy, international security and global affairs.

MY STORY

Learning, leadership
and international dialogue.

I was born on 16 May 2007. I studied Economics at TUIT Academic Lyceum, where I became involved in student leadership, debate and Model United Nations.

After graduating, I was admitted to Ege University to study International Relations with the support of the Türkiye Scholarships programme.

My interest in international affairs has developed through more than 40 Model United Nations conferences, where I have taken on roles including delegate, rapporteur, chair and organiser. I also served as Secretary-General of ALTUIT MUN, led the ALTUIT Students’ Council and founded Argument Arena Debate Club.

I have completed UNODC’s Advanced Anti-Corruption: Prevention of Corruption course and UNITAR’s Arms Trade Treaty Annual Reporting Course. These experiences continue to shape my interest in diplomacy, multilateral cooperation and global governance.
